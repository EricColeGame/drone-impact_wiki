*   **Touch Sensitivity Calibration:** Precise reticle adjustments are required when targeting moving convoys with the 25mm Gatling gun. Set horizontal tracking sensitivity 10% lower than vertical tracking to reduce aim drift.
*   **Audio Visual Cues:** Keep sound effects high to hear incoming missile warning tones and ground troop distress signals clearly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I play Drone Impact offline without any Wi-Fi or data connection?
Most modern drone combat simulators require an active internet handshake upon startup to authenticate user profiles, cloud saves, and energy systems. While individual mission rendering runs locally on your device hardware, prolonged **Drone Impact offline** play is usually restricted until your device syncs with game servers.

### How do I stop my drone weapons from overheating in intense battles?
Manage your firing cadence by using short, controlled bursts with the 25mm autocannon instead of holding down the trigger. Alternating between rocket pods, guided missiles, and your primary turret prevents thermal lockouts during heavy enemy assaults.

### Why do high-level enemy units absorb so much damage?
In many free-to-play military shooters, community reports highlight that enemy infantry and armored vehicle health pools scale aggressively in later chapters. Upgrading your weapon's armor penetration stats, caliber size, and reload speed ensures your damage output keeps pace with advanced campaign stages.

### Do I lose campaign mission rewards if I lose connection mid-battle?
If your network drops while playing a mission, the local engine allows you to finish the active firefight. However, you will need to reconnect to the network at the debrief screen so the server can record your earned currency, weapon scraps, and stage completion.
