## Step-by-Step Walkthrough: Beating the Interception Scenario

The Interception mission is widely considered the toughest challenge within the trial release. Follow this structured roadmap to secure an S-Rank clear:

```
[Phase 1: Pre-Flight Config] --> [Phase 2: Low-Altitude Approach] --> [Phase 3: EMP Initiation] --> [Phase 4: Kinetic Sweep]
```

### 1. Pre-Flight Configuration
Equip the **Striker Fighter (F-4)** chassis with one **Kinetic Auto-Cannon**, one **Micro-Missile Pod**, and the **EMP Disruptor**. Allocate 60% of auxiliary power routing into thrusters and 40% into weapon cooling systems.

### 2. Low-Altitude Ingress
Begin the match by dropping below 15 meters altitude immediately. Flying low breaks long-range line-of-sight locks from enemy escort fighters. Use canyon walls and valleys to close the gap between your strike team and the target formation.

### 3. The EMP Strike
Once within 40 meters of the primary target, trigger your EMP Disruptor. This temporarily disables the target's countermeasure systems and stalls their thrusters for 3.5 seconds.

### 4. Kinetic Alpha Strike
Unload a complete volley of Micro-Missiles while the target is immobilized, following up immediately with sustained Kinetic Auto-Cannon fire into the exposed propulsion vents. Break off immediately to deploy chaff countermeasures before escort units can return fire.

## Advanced Combat Tactics and Tips

Based on community reports and player experiences during the current testing phase, several subtle flight mechanics can dramatically elevate your performance in the **Drone Impact demo**:

1. **Master Inertial Drift:** Cut your primary thrusters mid-turn while pitching hard to pivot your nose toward pursuers without losing directional momentum.
2. **Thermal Management over Raw Firepower:** Sustained firing overheats light chassis quickly. Fire weapons in controlled 2-second bursts to preserve shield recharge cycles.
3. **Exploit Environmental Hazards:** Hostile units struggle with sudden radar loss. Lure pursuing interceptor squads into dense industrial zones or electromagnetic storm pockets to break their missile locks naturally.
4. **Prioritize Radar Relay Stations:** In infiltration scenarios, destroying communication antennas prevents local outposts from broadcasting distress calls to nearby heavy strike forces.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What content is included in the Drone Impact demo?
The trial version includes three combat missions (Tactical Infiltration, Area Defense, Target Interception), a full Free-Flight Sandbox, three playable chassis classes, and a curated selection of kinetic, energy, and explosive weapon systems.

### Does progress from the preview carry over to the final release?
According to developer announcements and community reports, cosmetic unlock badges and flight achievement records will transfer to the full launch, but weapon tier unlocks and chassis upgrade trees will reset for overall game balance.

### Can I play the trial preview with a flight stick or HOTAS controller?
Yes. The build features native input mapping support for standard keyboards, gamepads, and popular flight stick/HOTAS peripherals, complete with customizable deadzone and sensitivity sliders.

### How do I fix stuttering issues while playing the demo?
Ensure graphics drivers are fully updated, switch the display mode to Exclusive Fullscreen, and reduce screen-space reflections and volumetric lighting settings in the options menu.
