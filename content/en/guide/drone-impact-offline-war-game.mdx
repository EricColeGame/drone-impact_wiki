### Weapon Thermal and Ammo Management

Every high-caliber system generates significant heat. Firing a 25mm Gatling gun continuously will cause overheating, leaving your drone defenseless during crucial counter-attacks. Rapid, controlled bursts maintain weapon readiness and ensure accuracy when tracking fast targets.

Missiles and heavy ordnance operate on limited payload capacities per sortie. Wasting precision-guided munitions on dispersed, low-threat foot soldiers drains resources you will desperately need against incoming armored fighting vehicles (AFVs) or hostile attack helicopters.

| System Type | Primary Role | Heat Buildup Rate | Ammo Conservation Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **25mm Gatling Gun** | Light vehicles & infantry | High (3–4 sec sustained) | Fire in 1.5-second bursts |
| **Hydra 70 Rockets** | Clustered units & light armor | Low (Salvo cooldown) | Group targets before launch |
| **Hellfire Missiles** | Heavy armor & fortifications | None (Ammo count locked) | Reserve strictly for high-armor targets |
| **EMP Systems** | Electronic & drone disruption | Cooldown-based | Deploy during heavy anti-air locks |

### Lead-Target Firing

Missiles and ballistic rounds have measurable travel time. When engaging moving convoys in your favorite **Drone Impact offline war game**, place your targeting reticle two vehicle lengths ahead of the target's current path to guarantee direct impact.

## FAQ: Drone Combat & Tactical Gameplay

### What is the most effective weapon against armored convoys?
Guided anti-tank missiles like the Hellfire provide the highest burst damage against heavy hulls. When facing clusters of lighter armored vehicles, a well-placed Hydra 70 rocket salvo can destroy multiple units in a single strike.

### How do I stop my drone from overheating during long missions?
Fire your primary autocannons in controlled 1- to 2-second bursts. Avoid holding down the trigger continuously, and swap to secondary missiles while your primary gun cools down.

### Why do higher campaign missions feel difficult to clear?
Player experience shows that enemy health scaling increases significantly in later levels. To overcome this, prioritize damage upgrades for your main weapon systems and invest in cooldown reductions for high-tier ordnance.

### Can I play this tactical drone combat experience without Wi-Fi?
Yes, a dedicated **Drone Impact offline war game** allows operators to experience single-player campaigns, tactical scenarios, and weapon progression without requiring an active internet connection.
